What this is & how to use it

A small personal-finance projection. Given your own figures it shows three things:

  • Income projected at your next few pay dates (assumes a fortnightly inflow plus a monthly one on the 10th), each as a $-thousands figure.
  • A 30-day rolling spend allowance (annual inflow minus savings target, spread over 30 days).
  • Offset-mortgage payoff time — when the loan clears if savings sweep into the offset each month.

It encodes specific assumptions (Australian-style fortnightly pay, an offset mortgage, savings swept into the offset), so it's most useful if your setup looks like that. It is not a general mortgage calculator.

Your numbers stay private. Every value lives in the part of the URL after the # (the fragment). Browsers never send the fragment to any server, so your figures stay in your bookmark and in your browser — they are not in this page, this repository, or any server log. Bookmark the generated link and open it whenever you want the numbers.

Any section appears only if you supply its inputs, so you can use just the loan calculator, for example.
